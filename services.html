Our Services

Committed toYour Elevator’s Performance

Complete Elevator Solutions

From Installation to Support

At HELIX ELEVATORS, we believe that elevator service should be reliable, transparent and convenient. From new elevator installation to regular maintenance, repairs and modernization, our experienced team manages every stage with attention to quality, safety and performance.

Commercial Elevators

Complete elevator solutions for offices, commercial buildings, shopping spaces and other high-traffic environments. Our services include installation, maintenance, repairs, modernization and technical support to keep your elevator operating safely and efficiently.

Industrial & Goods Elevators

Designed to support demanding environments and heavier loads, our industrial elevator services focus on durability, safety and dependable performance. Our technical team provides installation, servicing and maintenance for goods and industrial lifting applications.

Residential Elevators

Comfort, safety and reliability are essential for residential mobility. We provide installation, maintenance, repair and modernization services for residential elevators, villas, apartments and multi-storey homes.

New Elevator Installation

From planning and site coordination to installation and commissioning, our team manages the complete elevator installation process. We focus on precise installation, safety compliance and smooth commissioning, helping your project move forward with confidence.

Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

Regular maintenance is essential for elevator safety and long-term performance. Our AMC services are designed to keep elevators operating smoothly through scheduled inspections, preventive maintenance and timely attention to potential issues.

Repairs & General Service

Unexpected elevator issues can affect convenience, productivity and building operations. Our repair and general service team works to identify problems efficiently and restore reliable operation with a strong focus on safety, quality and long-term performance.
