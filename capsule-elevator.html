Capsule Lift

Capsule elevators significantly enhance the aesthetics of the elevation. Be it a premium residential space, commercial project or a hotel, capsule elevators add lots of value to economic success. Its wide view/ panoramic view provides an exceptional visual experience to the end user. Helix Elevators offers car cabins with exclusive interiors and shapes as per project demand. The company uses sophisticated mechanisms in these elevators making them a preferred choice for premium projects. In addition, they are extremely economical to operate and require very low maintenance.

These elevators can also be made using full glass doors making it a luxurious and unique preposition. Depending on the project design and machine room location, the lifts can be traction or hydraulic.
